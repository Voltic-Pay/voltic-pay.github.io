Built for paying, not trading

Self-custodial by default. Voltic never holds your keys or routes a payment through a company.

  • True P2P privacy

    Payments go directly between two wallets. Nobody logs your spending.

  • Zero middleman fees

    No 2% processor cut. What you send is what they get.

  • KYC-free

    No ID checks or onboarding forms.

  • One network

    Arbitrum (an Ethereum layer 2) only, so it stays simple.

  • Full self-custody

    Only your seed phrase controls the wallet.

  • Bring your wallet

    Import your seed phrase and your balance follows.

What's in the app

  • Tap or scan

    Tap for the familiar contactless feel, or scan a code when NFC isn't an option.

  • Hybrid offline NFC

    Sign a payment with no connection. The merchant's phone broadcasts it once it's online.

  • Material 3 Expressive

    A bold, bank-grade interface, not a spreadsheet with a wallet bolted on.

  • Biometric-gated backup

    Your seed phrase and other sensitive actions sit behind a fingerprint or face check.

Up and running in four steps

  1. Download the APK from the GitHub releases page. Android will warn you it's from outside the Play Store; that's expected for an early build.
  2. Create a wallet in the app, or restore one with an existing seed phrase.
  3. Fund it with ETH on Arbitrum. Bridge or swap into the account address on your dashboard.
  4. Tap or scan to pay. That's the whole app.

Build it yourself

# clone and build from source
git clone https://github.com/Voltic-Pay/Voltic-App.git
cd Voltic-App
# add your Arbiscan key for tx history (optional)
echo "ARBISCAN_API_KEY=your_key" >> local.properties
./gradlew assembleDebug

Needs Android Studio Ladybug+, JDK 17+, and an NFC-capable device to test tap-to-pay. To deploy your own version of the payment contract, see the README inside /contracts.
